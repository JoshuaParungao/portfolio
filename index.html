Overall System Architecture & Scope

Arunafeltz is a custom, commercial-grade cloud Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) and Point of Sale (POS) system unifying all retail, service, and supply chain operations for computer hardware enterprises across physical branches (San Fernando & Dau, Pampanga).

The platform integrates 12 mission-critical operational domains into one cohesive system: fast POS counter cashiering, unit-level serialized barcode tracking, inter-branch logistics, supplier POs & receiving, custom PC building, technical repair job orders, AR credit/installment financing, warranty claims, shift cash box auditing, sales commissions, and executive P&L intelligence.

Privacy & Access Security: Features a secured, locked login gateway enforcing 5-tier Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and database-level branch isolation to guarantee 100% data security, commercial privacy, and operational integrity.

Financial, Cash & Transactional Ledgers
Automated Double-Entry Principles, Drawer Balancing & Real-Time Accounting
ACID-COMPLIANT LEDGERS
1. Split-Payment Sales Ledger
POS & Sales

Tracks multi-tender payments per receipt (Cash, GCash, Bank Transfer, PDC/Cheque, AR Credit). Records exact transaction timestamps, assigned cashier, sales agent attribution, and discount structures.

  • • Audited Voids: Non-destructive cancellations with reverse stock & cash journaling
  • • Multi-Tier Pricing: Automated tier selection (Tier 1 Cash Discount to Tier 5 Wholesale)
2. Accounts Receivable (AR) & Credit Ledger
Financing & Credit

Customer-level credit lines, installment schedules (Month 3 to 12), and historical amortization ledgers for in-house credit and 3rd-party financing (Home Credit, Credit Card).

  • • Remittance & Float: Shop Net, CC/Tax Float, and settled float reconciliation
  • • SOA Engine: Live Statement of Account generation & Bad Debt declaration
3. Cash Box & Drawer Shift Ledger
Cash Balancing

Live cash drawer monitoring from morning opening float to day-end cash reconciliation. Direct cash linkage logs all cash-in (sales, collections) and cash-out (expenses, petty cash).

  • • Variance Ledger: Over/Short discrepancy logging with reason and supervisor sign-off
  • • Shift Handovers: Drawer snapshots and auditable mid-day cash adjustments
4. Serialized Stock Movement Ledger
WMS Inventory

Every unit-level serial number is tracked chronologically from PO receiving, transfer dispatch, sales deduction, repair pull-out, to customer warranty replacement.

  • • Zero Leakage: Prevents duplicate serial ingestion and phantom inventory
  • • Stock Adjustments: Physical count reconciliation with audited write-offs
5. Employee Commissions & Incentive Ledger
Staff Payroll / Cuts

Dynamic automated commission engine calculating cuts per sales agent, repair technician labor fee, and custom PC builder assembly fee based on branch settings.

  • • Claim Approvals: Strict claim submission, admin review, and payout disbursement
  • • Auto-Reversals: Automatic commission clawbacks when sales are voided
6. Immutable System Audit Ledger
Enterprise Governance

Complete, tamper-evident audit trail logging every write mutation: user ID, role, branch, IP address, timestamp, table, and full JSON before/after state snapshots.

  • • Security Compliance: Tracks price changes, stock overrides, voids, and logins
  • • Forensic Accountability: Transparent trace for multi-branch store operations

Enterprise Engineering Highlights & Data Integrity
  • Atomic Database Transactions: Leveraged Prisma interactive transactions (prisma.$transaction) ensuring multi-step inventory deductions, serial status transitions, payment ledgers, and technician incentives succeed or fail together atomically.
  • Strict Multi-Branch Isolation & RBAC: Enforced backend authorization restricting branch-scoped personnel (Cashiers, Technicians, Admins) strictly to their active branch, while allowing Super Owners enterprise-wide governance.
  • Concurrency Control & Race Condition Prevention: Implemented row-level locking (SELECT ... FOR UPDATE) on serialized items, transfer dispatches, and credit collections to prevent race conditions during high-volume cashier traffic.
  • High-Throughput Keyboard-First UX: Engineered lightweight debounced typeahead autocompletes with arrow key/Enter navigation and Omnisearch (Ctrl+K), maximizing throughput for cashier and inventory encoders.
