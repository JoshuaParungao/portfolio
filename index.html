Engineering Rationale: Why an Offline-First LAN Suite?

Unlike traditional web-based retail systems, retail drugstores require zero-downtime fault tolerance. Internet service outages (ISP drops, DNS failures, latency spikes) immediately halt customer checkouts, leading to lost revenue and customer frustration.

The Engineering Solution: MedC was engineered as an on-premise distributed LAN suite. The central backend server, high-speed SQLite database engine, and frontend Electron workstations run entirely within the physical building's Gigabit network.

Key Architectural Advantages:
• Microsecond Barcode Response: Immediate barcode lookup with zero internet round-trip ping.
• Zero Cloud Overhead: Eliminates perpetual monthly cloud hosting bills and third-party SaaS vendor lock-in.
• Data Sovereignty: All customer ledgers, daily sales, and supplier costs remain securely inside store premises.

Mission-Critical Pharmaceutical Business Logic & Data Pipeline
1. Dual-Unit Conversion Matrix (PCS ↔ BOX)

Pharmacy products are purchased in bulk boxes but sold in either full boxes or individual blister pieces. MEDC implements an intelligent conversion matrix:

• Box Price Governance: System validates that Selling Price per Box is strictly configured whenever box packaging is enabled.
• Cart Switching: Cashiers can switch selling units between PCS and BOX on the fly without re-scanning.
• Atomic Base Deduction: During checkout, box sales are converted atomically to base piece quantities, ensuring physical inventory counts remain 100% accurate.
2. Batch-Level FIFO Expiry Quarantine

Pharmaceutical regulations require rigorous expiry management. MEDC enforces First-In, First-Out (FIFO) stock deductions while maintaining granular batch visibility:

• Real-time Expiry Status: Automatically flags batches as VALID, EXPIRING_SOON, or EXPIRED.
• Quarantine Protection: Expired batches are automatically locked and prevented from being dispensed at cashier counters.
• 1-Click Quick Dispose: Pharmacists can dispose of expired stocks directly from the batch modal, creating an audited shrinkage log and resolving alert counters.
3. Quotation Studio & Multi-Format Export

Medical supply distributors require frequent formal pricing quotations for clinics and wholesale buyers:

• In-Line Price Overrides: Cashiers can customize negotiated prices and line-item quantities dynamically.
• Unlisted Line Items: Ability to quote custom non-inventory supplies and equipment directly.
• Triple-Format Exports: Generates Word (.docx), Vector PDF with clean typography, and formatted Excel (.xlsx via ExcelJS) with automated column widths and formulas.
4. Double-Entry Shift Balancing & Auditing

Guarantees total cashier accountability and cash drawer integrity across morning and afternoon shifts:

• Cashier Shift Life Cycle: Open Shift starting cash, mid-shift cash-ins/cash-outs, and End-of-Shift drawer count.
• Overage / Shortage Calculation: Immediate comparison between expected register drawer cash and physical cash counts.
• Audit Trails: Automated security logging for every price edit, stock adjustment, voided sale, and user permission event.
